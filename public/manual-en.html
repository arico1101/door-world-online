TOBIRA 🌍 World

A life board game of money and choices
📱 one device each 👥 2–6 players ⏱ 60 / 25 min
🏆 The ranking isn't decided by money, but by ♥ Happiness.
♥ = how often you chose with your own will. Money and ★ Learning are the "keys" that make it possible.
❓ Why start at age 6? — Because the base of a life is built in childhood. You don't choose where you're born. Same dice, same board for everyone — yet what each player can see is different. You'll find out why after the game.
📲 How to joineveryone opens the same URL on their own device
1The host presses "Create a room" and gets a room code
2Everyone else enters the code and a name, then "Join room"
3The host picks a board: Full (age 6–35, 2–6 players, ~60 min) or Short (age 6–25, 1–4 players, 25 min)
4Press Start once everyone is in. No joining after the start. Disconnected? Reopen the same page to get back in

🔑 Doors & keys

  • Choosing at a glowing Door earns ♥ Happiness
  • Good options need keys (money / ★ Learning). Not enough → 🔒
  • Depending on your family, some options show only as "？？？" — invisible
  • A no-key (¥0) path can always be chosen, even in debt
  • ★ and ♥ stay hidden until you choose — you see the price, not the value
  • Before you choose, say out loud why
