What kind of game?

You're born somewhere in the world,
and walk 29 years — from age 6 to 35

You don't choose where you're born.
Same dice, same board for everyone.
But what you can see is different for each person — that's this game.

The most important rule

The ranking isn't decided by money,
but by ♥ Happiness

Your ♥ count ＝ how many of life's options you held — and chose with your own will.
💰 Money and 📚 ★ Learning are the "keys" that open them.

🗺 Square types
💰 Work

You earn pay: base wage ＋ ★ Learning × multiplier ＋ allowance. Both the wage and the multiplier depend on where you were born…!

💸 Expenses

Living costs money. Pay the fixed amount

⚡ Events

Draw one card. Could be lucky, could be an accident

📚 Learning

A chance to invest in yourself. Even ¥0 options let you learn

🚪 Doors

Glowing squares ＝ life's crossroads. Land on one and choose your path

🔑 Doors and keys
1

Good options need keys (money / ★ Learning). Without enough, the door is 🔒 locked

2

A no-key (¥0) path is always available

3

You see the money cost before choosing. But ★ and ♥ stay hidden until you choose — you can know the price, not the value

🎓 Scholarships
1

"Continue with a scholarship / aid group" is a loan (¥480k total). Every payday, ¥60k is deducted — you keep repaying until 35

2

If you know the right support, you may find a scholarship you never repay. Knowing, by itself, is power
